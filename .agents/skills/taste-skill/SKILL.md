# Taste UI Guidelines for MyBantu

## Art Direction & Palette
* **Theme**: Modern trust-first service platform (Navy/Slate, Crisp Emerald accent for trust/guarantee, warm neutrals).
* **Typography**: Clear hierarchy with tight leading on display headings and relaxed leading on body text.
* **Layout**: Asymmetric balancing, generous whitespace, avoiding generic 3-card grids.
* **Surfaces**: Subtle layered shadows (`shadow-sm` to `shadow-md`), thin borders (`border-slate-200/80`), translucent glassmorphism for sticky navs.
